Who We Are

A Department Unlike Any Other in Ghana

Founded in 1976 as a Diploma programme, the then Department of Integrated Rural Art and Industry (IRAI) — now the Department of Indigenous Art and Technology (DIAT) at the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST) — is Ghana's pioneering institution for indigenous art education, uniquely combining Academia, commercial craft production, and active Community engagement under one roof.

Our seven programmes ground every student in Product Design thinking from Year 1, equipping them with Computer-Aided Design (CAD) tools and design methodology before guiding them into mastery of two of six indigenous craft disciplines.

Community Engagement

What We Teach

Seven Academic Programmes

Every student begins with Product Design — learning to envision, draw, and digitally prototype their ideas before committing to their chosen craft discipline.

📐
Product Design

The foundational strand taught across all years. Students use CAD software to visualise and specify artworks before production — bridging indigenous craft with contemporary design technology.
