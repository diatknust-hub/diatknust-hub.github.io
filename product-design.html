Product Design

Before any student throws clay, cuts leather, or forges metal, they design it digitally. Product Design runs across all four years — the intellectual and technical spine connecting every craft discipline at DIAT.

4 Years Across All Years
Curriculum

Skills & Techniques

Core skills developed across the four-year programme, building progressively from foundational technique to advanced, professional-level practice.

01

Computer-Aided Design (CAD)

Using industry-standard software to design objects and products before physical production — from concept sketches to full 3D models.

02

Design Thinking & Research

User-centred research, problem definition, ideation, prototyping, and iteration methodologies applied to craft product development.

03

Materials Science

Understanding the properties and limitations of clay, leather, metal, fibre, rattan, and wood as design materials.

04

Technical Drawing & Specification

Producing professional technical drawings, measurements, and production specifications for craft objects.

05

Prototype Development

Translating digital designs into physical prototypes using workshop tools, assessing fit, function, and aesthetics.

06

Commercial Design Practice

Understanding market contexts, costing, client briefs, and the business of bringing craft objects to market.

Careers

Where This Takes You

DIAT graduates specialising in Product Design enter a wide range of creative, commercial, and academic careers — equipped with both deep craft expertise and modern design professionalism.

📰

Design Consultant

Working with manufacturers, craft enterprises, and NGOs as an indigenous craft design specialist.

🌐

Product Developer

Developing new craft-informed products for local and international markets with cultural relevance.

🎓

Academic / Researcher

Teaching and researching at university level within craft design, material culture, or design education.

🌌

Craft Entrepreneur

Founding your own studio, workshop, or craft brand combining digital design capability with hand production.
