Rattan & Bamboo

Sustainable natural materials woven, bent, and constructed into furniture, decorative objects, and architectural installations. One of the most environmentally significant disciplines at DIAT, connecting contemporary sustainability concerns to ancient Ghanaian material culture.

4 Years B.A. Programme
Curriculum

Skills & Techniques

Core skills developed across the four-year programme, building progressively from foundational technique to advanced, professional-level practice.

01

Material Preparation

Splitting, soaking, and conditioning rattan and bamboo canes for various craft applications — from delicate weaving to structural construction.

02

Weaving & Plaiting

Over-under, hexagonal, and decorative weave patterns for panels, baskets, and surface decoration.

03

Furniture Construction

Frame-building, joinery, and structural techniques for chairs, tables, shelving, and storage using rattan and bamboo.

04

Bending & Forming

Steam-bending and cold-forming techniques for creating curved structural forms in both rattan and bamboo.

05

Surface Finishing

Varnishing, oiling, staining, and lacquering techniques for protecting and beautifying finished rattan and bamboo works.

06

Sustainable Design Practice

Understanding the environmental lifecycle of rattan and bamboo, sourcing ethics, and sustainable production management.

Careers

Where This Takes You

DIAT graduates specialising in Rattan & Bamboo enter a wide range of creative, commercial, and academic careers — equipped with both deep craft expertise and modern design professionalism.

🍋

Sustainable Furniture Designer

Designing and producing rattan and bamboo furniture for Ghana's growing interior design and hospitality sectors.

🎓

Craft Education Specialist

Teaching rattan and bamboo crafts at vocational, polytechnic, and university levels.

🌿

Environmental Craft Practitioner

Working with environmental organisations and NGOs to promote sustainable craft practice and material sourcing.

🌌

Craft Export Business

Exporting handcrafted rattan and bamboo goods to international markets, hotels, and design buyers.
