Academic Programmes

Six Craft Disciplines. One Design Spine.

DIAT offers the B.A. (IRAI) degree across six indigenous craft disciplines, all underpinned by Product Design and computer-aided design thinking.

Programme Structure

Built on Product Design Thinking

Every student at DIAT, regardless of which craft discipline they specialise in, learns Product Design across all four years of the programme. This is the intellectual and technical spine — students design digitally before making by hand.

In Years 1 and 2, students study a broad foundation covering multiple disciplines. In Years 3 and 4, they specialise in one discipline alongside an extended research project and community internship.

Programme Facts

→Degree: B.A. (IRAI) — Bachelor of Arts
→Duration: 4 years
→Postgraduate: MPhil Integrated Art
→Accreditation: KNUST / NAB Ghana
→Internship: 3 months (Year 3)
