Who We Are

A Department Unlike Any Other in Ghana

Founded in 1976, DIAT is the only university department in Ghana dedicated entirely to indigenous craft disciplines — taught as living, professionally viable creative practice, not cultural history.

Read Our Story →
Academic Programmes

Six Craft Disciplines. One Design Spine.

Clay, Fibres, Leather, Metal, Rattan, and Wood — six disciplines, all underpinned by Product Design and a shared CAD-to-craft methodology.
