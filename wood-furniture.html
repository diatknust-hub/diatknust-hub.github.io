🪓 B.A. (IRAI) Specialisation

Wood & Furniture

Joinery, carving, turning, and furniture-making rooted in Ghana's tradition of carved stools, ceremonial doors, and royal objects. Students work with Ghana's rich hardwood species alongside contemporary furniture design and production methods.

4 Years B.A. Programme
Curriculum

Skills & Techniques

Core skills developed across the four-year programme, building progressively from foundational technique to advanced, professional-level practice.

01

Hand Carving

Chisels, gouges, and mallets for traditional and decorative carved works — from figurative sculpture to architectural ornamentation.

02

Joinery

Mortise-and-tenon, dovetail, biscuit, and other structural wood joints for furniture and architectural timber work.

03

Wood Turning

Lathe operation for producing bowls, vessels, spindles, and turned components for furniture and decorative objects.

04

Furniture Making

Design, construction, and finishing of seating, tables, storage, and case furniture using Ghanaian hardwoods and sustainable species.

05

Surface Finishing

Sanding, staining, oiling, waxing, and lacquering for achieving professional wood surface finishes.

06

Traditional Stool Carving

The Ashanti royal stool as a technical and cultural study — understanding its forms, symbolism, and carving techniques.

Careers

Where This Takes You

DIAT graduates specialising in Wood & Furniture enter a wide range of creative, commercial, and academic careers — equipped with both deep craft expertise and modern design professionalism.

🪓

Furniture Designer-Maker

Designing and producing fine furniture drawing on Ghana's rich wood culture for domestic and export markets.

🎓

Wood Arts Educator

Teaching woodworking and furniture design at technical institutes and universities in Ghana.

⛪

Architectural Woodwork Specialist

Producing carved panels, doors, staircase elements, and architectural timber work for Ghana's construction sector.

🌌

Wood Craft Studio

Operating a studio producing culturally distinctive wood objects, furniture, and carved works for local and international buyers.
