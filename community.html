DIAT Tops Group A at CABE’s 75th Anniversary Quiz

📅 23 June 2026 🏆 CABE 75th Anniversary Quiz 🥇 Group A Winner

As part of the College of Art and Built Environment (CABE) 75th Anniversary celebrations, departments across the college faced off in an Inter-Departmental Quiz Competition. DIAT was drawn into Group A alongside the Department of Publishing Studies and the Department of Land Economy, with the elimination round held on 23rd June 2026 at the New CABE Building.

A solid showing from a small department — and a proud moment heading into the next stage of CABE’s 75th Anniversary quiz competitions.

When Leather Met Colour

📅 June 2026 🏛 Leather Technology Section 🤝 Second Annual Visit

Under the spirit of Sankofa — the Ghanaian symbol that teaches it is no fault to return to the past and carry forward what is valuable — students from Sacramento State University joined DIAT’s Leather Technology section at KNUST for a second year running, learning how vegetable-tanned leather is prepared for production from raw hide through to a finished, ready-to-work surface.

The session went further than tanning alone — visitors were introduced to dye techniques more commonly seen in the Fibres & Fabrics section: tie-and-dye, marbling, knotting, and spray application through stencils — each adapted here to give leather a distinct, painterly finish.

The day closed with visitors laying their freshly dyed, still-wet leather over carved wooden masks, pressing the hide into the mould to lift every detail of the carving before the pieces were left to dry — transforming flat, painted leather into dimensional, wearable art.

The Partnership

The Mastercard Foundation Partnership

In partnership with the Mastercard Foundation, DIAT operates Ghana’s most comprehensive indigenous craft internship programme. Year 3 students spend three months embedded with artisan communities across all regions of Ghana.

The internship is not observational — students actively contribute to production, document techniques, and formally attribute the knowledge systems of their host artisan communities in reports that become part of DIAT’s permanent archive.

Programme At a Glance

→Duration: 3 months (end of Year 3)
→Coverage: Nationwide across Ghana
→Partner: Mastercard Foundation
→Output: Formal craft knowledge attribution reports
→Disciplines: All 6 craft sections
The Process

How the Internship Works

01

Community Placement

Students are placed with artisan communities across Ghana’s regions matching their craft discipline specialisation.

02

Active Production

Students contribute directly to artisan production — not as observers but as working members of the craft community.

03

Knowledge Documentation

Each student formally documents the craft knowledge systems encountered, creating attribution reports for DIAT’s archive.
