Internship & Outreach

Community & Internship Programme

DIAT’s three-month nationwide internship embeds students with artisan communities across Ghana, formally attributing and preserving living indigenous craft knowledge.

The Partnership

The Mastercard Foundation Partnership

In partnership with the Mastercard Foundation, DIAT operates Ghana’s most comprehensive indigenous craft internship programme. Year 3 students spend three months embedded with artisan communities across all regions of Ghana.

The internship is not observational — students actively contribute to production, document techniques, and formally attribute the knowledge systems of their host artisan communities in reports that become part of DIAT’s permanent archive.

Programme At a Glance

→Duration: 3 months (end of Year 3)
→Coverage: Nationwide across Ghana
→Partner: Mastercard Foundation
→Output: Formal craft knowledge attribution reports
→Disciplines: All 6 craft sections
The Process

How the Internship Works

01

Community Placement

Students are placed with artisan communities across Ghana’s regions matching their craft discipline specialisation.

02

Active Production

Students contribute directly to artisan production — not as observers but as working members of the craft community.

03

Knowledge Documentation

Each student formally documents the craft knowledge systems encountered, creating attribution reports for DIAT’s archive.

Community Impact

Preserving Ghana’s Living
Craft Heritage

Every internship cohort adds to a growing archive of formally attributed indigenous craft knowledge — a lasting contribution to Ghana’s cultural heritage record.
