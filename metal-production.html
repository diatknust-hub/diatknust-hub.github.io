Home / Programmes / Metal Production
⚙ B.A. (IRAI) Specialisation

Metal Production

Lost-wax casting, forging, welding, and fabrication — connected to the Ashanti goldsmithing heritage that shaped the artistic identity of one of Ghana's greatest royal cultures. Students work in precious metals, iron, aluminium, and mixed media.

4 Years B.A. Programme
Curriculum

Skills & Techniques

Core skills developed across the four-year programme, building progressively from foundational technique to advanced, professional-level practice.

01

Lost-wax Casting (Cire Perdue)

The traditional Ashanti goldsmithing technique — carving wax models, investing in clay, casting in molten metal.

02

Forging & Smithing

Shaping metal by heat and hammer at the anvil — a skill rooted in both Ghanaian blacksmithing and European craft traditions.

03

Welding & Fabrication

MIG, TIG, and arc welding techniques for joining metal components in sculptural and architectural metalwork.

04

Jewellery Making

Designing and producing rings, pendants, earrings, and ceremonial jewellery in gold, silver, and mixed metals.

05

Metal Casting

Sand-casting, die-casting, and investment casting for functional and decorative metal objects at production scale.

06

Surface Treatment

Filing, grinding, polishing, patination, and coatings for achieving professional metal surface finishes.

Careers

Where This Takes You

DIAT graduates specialising in Metal Production enter a wide range of creative, commercial, and academic careers — equipped with both deep craft expertise and modern design professionalism.

⚙

Jewellery Designer

Designing and producing fine jewellery drawing on Ashanti goldsmithing traditions for local and export markets.

🎓

Metal Arts Educator

Teaching metal production and fabrication at technical institutes and universities.

🌍

Architectural Metal Fabricator

Producing metal gates, railings, panels, and architectural metalwork for Ghana's construction sector.

🌌

Goldsmith & Craftsperson

Maintaining the living tradition of Ashanti goldsmithing for ceremonial, cultural, and commercial purposes.
