👜 B.A. (IRAI) Specialisation

Leather Technology

Tanning, pattern-making, stitching, and finishing — from West African leather traditions to contemporary market-ready goods. Students work with locally sourced hides and traditional dyeing methods alongside modern production techniques.

4 Years B.A. Programme
Curriculum

Skills & Techniques

Core skills developed across the four-year programme, building progressively from foundational technique to advanced, professional-level practice.

01

Hide Preparation

Identifying, grading, and conditioning leather for craft use — from raw hide to finished material.

02

Pattern-making & Cutting

Drafting, scaling, and cutting patterns for bags, footwear, belts, accessories, and architectural leather works.

03

Hand-stitching & Sewing

Saddle-stitch, running stitch, and machine techniques for joining leather components with professional finish.

04

Dyeing & Surface Finishing

Natural and synthetic dye application, burnishing, waxing, and surface treatment for aesthetic and protective finishes.

05

Footwear Construction

Last-based shoemaking from Ghanaian sandal traditions to contemporary fashion and protective footwear.

06

Product Development

Designing and prototyping original leather goods collections combining Ghanaian aesthetic language with market requirements.

Careers

Where This Takes You

DIAT graduates specialising in Leather Technology enter a wide range of creative, commercial, and academic careers — equipped with both deep craft expertise and modern design professionalism.

👜

Leather Goods Designer

Designing and producing bags, accessories, and footwear for Ghanaian and export markets.

🎓

Craft Technology Educator

Teaching leather technology at vocational, polytechnic, and university levels across Ghana.

🍎

Heritage Craft Practitioner

Maintaining and teaching traditional West African leather techniques for cultural preservation.

🌌

Leather Studio Entrepreneur

Operating a studio producing premium, culturally distinctive leather goods for domestic and international buyers.
